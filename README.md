# From L¹ to Lᵖ: Supplementary Material

Supplementary material related to *On Lᵖ density of self similar measures and Bernoulli convolutions* by **Samuel Kittle and Constantin Kogler**.

This repository collects research notes on quantitative criteria for Lᵖ and continuous densities of Bernoulli convolutions, building on the methods and results of the main paper. The notes were developed with AI assistance.

## Contents

- [A cubic criterion for continuous algebraic Bernoulli convolutions](bernoulli_algebraic_cubic.pdf): a cubic sufficient condition in terms of the algebraic degree and Mahler measure, yielding an L² density for $\nu_{\lambda^2}$ and a continuous density for $\nu_\lambda$.
